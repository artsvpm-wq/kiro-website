📜 Epigraphical Context

This memorial hero stone (Naṭukal) was discovered in Mothakkal (Chengam taluk, Tamil Nadu). It commemorates the hero Vinralai Vadukan who died defending his community against a predatory tiger. Inscribed in early Tamil Vatteluttu script, it dates to the 32nd regnal year of Pallava King Mahendravarman I (622 CE).
